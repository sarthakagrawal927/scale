# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineers learning systems design through repeated diagnosis and engineering decisions.

## Product Purpose

Scale helps engineers develop systems judgment by growing a simulated company, diagnosing bottlenecks, comparing interventions, and observing their consequences.

## Positioning

A persistent engineering game with a shared simulation engine for company and sandbox. The owner explicitly approved standalone Scale, separate from Wars, and authorized reuse of Breakscale code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Local-first browser experience. Fictional business economics. Measured simulation metrics, not real infrastructure forecasts. No cloud access or production systems. No deployment authorized. Start with compute, MySQL, query optimization, Redis, engineering time and an isolated sandbox. Further progression remains an expansion.

## Product Principles

Multiple valid choices; workload changes their value. Explain observed causes. Protect offline progress. Do not equate game progress with demonstrated mastery.

## Confirmed Direction

The owner requested a smooth, beautiful, professionally crafted interface and directed implementation after reviewing the playable-loop scope. The aesthetic implementation is agent-selected.
